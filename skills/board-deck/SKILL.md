# Seed stage board deck

Draft a ten-slide deck. Output is a draft for the founder to review. It is not financial, legal or investment advice. Never invent figures. Use placeholders or clearly labeled illustrative numbers.

## Slides

1. Highlights and lowlights: three wins with the number, three misses with cause, fix, owner and date.
2. Product: releases tied to a metric, top three roadmap items. For AI products add output quality, model or provider dependence, compute cost per customer.
3. Go-to-market: pipeline, conversion, sales cycle, retention, best and worst channel.
4. Financial summary: cash in bank, net burn, runway, revenue, gross margin, each defined on the slide.
5. Budget vs actual: budget, actual, variance in dollars and percent, one line of cause per material variance.
6. KPI scorecard: prior quarter, current, target, status in words. Include inference and GPU cost as a share of revenue.
7. Hiring and team: role, status, target start, what the hire changes.
8. Risks: four, ranked, with likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner.
9. Decisions and asks: options, recommendation, date, named person.
10. Appendix: definitions of every metric and formula.

## Definitions to state on slide 4

- Net burn: gross burn minus revenue. Gross burn is total monthly expenses.
- Runway: cash in bank divided by net burn.
- Gross margin: (revenue minus cost of revenue) divided by revenue. Say whether inference counts as cost of revenue. Recommend confirming that policy with the accountant, then applying it every period.
- Variance: actual minus budget. A positive variance on a cost line is unfavorable.

## Process

Close the month first. Fill slides 4 to 6 from closed numbers. Check the arithmetic. Write slide 1 last, leading with the number that missed. Send at least 48 hours before the meeting. Anything that does not drive a decision goes in the appendix.

## Output

Start with "DRAFT for human review. Not financial advice." Then the ten slides with fields filled or flagged.
