# Seed data room (back office)

Cover the back-office records only: finance, payroll and contractors, and cap table and entity. Deck, product and customer evidence are out of scope.

Output is a draft for human review. It is not legal or tax advice. Legal documents belong to the company's lawyer. Tax documents belong to the tax preparer. If asked a legal or tax question, say so and stop.

## Method

Ask what already exists. Mark each item: final, draft, missing. For every missing item give an owner and a date. Name gaps on the index. Never hide a gap and never backdate a document.

## Checklist (23 items)

**Finance (8):** monthly P&L; balance sheet and cash flow by month; cash balance and runway with the net burn used; revenue by customer and month tied to billing; compute and model spend by provider; credit and prepaid schedule with expiry dates; month-end bank statements with account numbers redacted; last twelve investor updates.

**Payroll and contractors (8):** headcount by role with start date and how each person is paid; monthly payroll total tied to the P&L; signed employment agreements; signed contractor agreements; contractor payments by month; W-9 or applicable W-8 on file for each contractor; 1099-NEC copies from the tax preparer if any; employer of record agreement and invoices if used.

**Cap table and entity (7):** dated master cap table; SAFEs and notes outstanding with terms; certificate of incorporation and bylaws; good standing certificate; founder agreements and IP assignment; option plan and grants if any; board and stockholder consents for each financing.

**Index and on request:** index with status, owner and date on every file; named gaps; tax returns, audits, leases and offer letters kept in a closed folder until an investor asks.

Keep twelve months of monthly history, or everything since incorporation.

## Folder layout

```
00 Index (open gaps, owners, dates)
01 Finance
02 Payroll and contractors
03 Cap table and entity
04 On request (closed until asked)
```

Status on every file: draft, executed or superseded. Any document should be two clicks away.

## Never include

Bank logins or credentials. Individual pay and benefit files beyond the payroll summary. Individual contractor invoices beyond the monthly record.

## Monthly upkeep

After each close: add the month to Finance, tie payroll and contractor totals to the P&L, update compute and credit schedules, file the sent investor update, date the cap table if an event occurred, update the index.

## Output

Start with "DRAFT for human review. Not legal or tax advice." Then the checklist with status, the folder index, and the gap list.
